Exam Pattern Reference

Mid — 30 Marks

Part A: 5 × 2M = 10M
Answer all 5 short questions.
Part B: 6 questions, attempt any 4.
Each Q further split into sub-parts.
4 × 5M = 20M

Sem — 60 Marks

Part A: 10 × 1M = 10M
Answer all 10, one-mark each.
Part B: 5 questions, attempt all.
Each Q has (a+b) or (c+d) choice.
a=b=c=d=5M per sub-question.

Sem Part B Rule

In Sem Part B each question gives options like:
Q1: a) or b)
Q1: c) or d)
You answer either a+b OR c+d — not a+c or b+d.
